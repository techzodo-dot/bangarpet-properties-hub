{# ======================= TRUST / SAFETY ======================= #}
Built on trust

How we keep every search safe

Property decisions are big decisions. We put checks in place so you can focus on finding the right home.

The "Verified" badge means identity documents were reviewed by our team. It is not a legal check of property title — always verify ownership documents before paying.

Manual listing review

Photos, prices and details are checked before a listing is published, and again when key details change.

Verified owners & brokers

Partners can submit ID and business documents, stored privately and seen only by our verification team.

Privacy controls

Owners decide who sees their phone number, and exact addresses stay hidden unless they choose to share them.

Report & act fast

Anything suspicious can be reported in one tap. Our team reviews every report and removes policy violations.
